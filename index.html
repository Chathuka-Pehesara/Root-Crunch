The Four Power Roots

Every pack of Root Crunch brings together four extraordinary tropical root vegetables, each bringing its own signature crunch, vibrant color, and wholesome nutrients.

🥕

Carrot

Daucus Carota

Thinly sliced and slow-baked to intensify their subtle natural sweetness and golden amber crunch.

  • Rich in Beta-Carotene & Vitamin A
  • Naturally sweet & vibrant amber color
  • Lightly crisped with sea salt
🪷

Lotus Root

Nelum Ala

The star of our blend. Iconic wheel-shaped slices with natural air channels that deliver an unbeatable crisp snap.

  • Distinctive lace wheel structure
  • High dietary fibre & plant minerals
  • Unrivalled snap and delicate texture
🥔

Kohila Ala

Elephant Foot Yam

A prized traditional Sri Lankan super-root. Rich in unique prebiotic fibres that support gut wellness.

  • Authentic Sri Lankan heritage root
  • Prebiotic gut health champion
  • Hearty, satisfying, savory crunch
🍠

Sweet Potato

Ipomoea Batatas

Golden-purple slices packed with complex carbohydrates and antioxidants for long-lasting energy.

  • Complex carb energy source
  • Rich in natural antioxidants
  • Creamy earthy sweet flavor profile

Artisanal Process

Sliced to Perfection,
Baked to Goodness

"Root Crunch is made from real vegetables sliced to perfection, lightly seasoned and baked to crunchy goodness. A wholesome snack you can feel good about."

Unlike ordinary potato chips that are deep-fried in excessive oil, our root vegetables are harvested directly from Sri Lankan family farms, washed, precisely sliced into thin cross-sections, tossed lightly in high-grade rice bran oil, and oven-baked under controlled heat.
