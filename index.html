"Root Crunch is made from real vegetables sliced to perfection, lightly seasoned and baked to crunchy goodness. A wholesome snack you can feel good about."

Unlike ordinary potato chips that are deep-fried in excessive oil, our root vegetables are harvested directly from Sri Lankan family farms, washed, precisely sliced into thin cross-sections, tossed lightly in high-grade rice bran oil, and oven-baked under controlled heat.
